Current projects

Document CI

DocFence

A local-first change-assurance CLI for Word documents and templates. It records stored content-block changes, hidden review surfaces, sensitivity-label, package digital-signature, Word protection, document-variable, DOCVARIABLE-field-reference, HYPERLINK-field-reference, direct Word hyperlink, DrawingML action and linked-picture markup, legacy VML shape-link, external-image, image-data hyperlink, linked-OLE and embedded-OLE, WordprocessingML linked-object-property and embedded-control-anchor markup, editable-range permission material, document-task and task-pane Office web-extension state, modern-comment metadata, document-property, mail-merge, content-control data-binding, external-source field, external-document dependency state, embedded payloads, and alternative-format imports without uploading or exposing document material in CI output.

Spreadsheet CI

FormulaFence

A local-first change-assurance CLI for Excel workbooks. It turns a binary spreadsheet diff into a reviewable account of formula changes, downstream impact, hidden controls, refresh behavior, policy failures, and portable browser review artifacts.

Spreadsheet research

Workbook Change Assurance Benchmark

An open, deterministic paired-workbook benchmark for tools that must review spreadsheet changes, show static impact, and state what they cannot establish. Its fixtures are generated from source, not copied from private models.

How I work

  • Evidence before hypeStart with a real workflow, test the uncomfortable cases, and show the limits.
  • Local control by defaultImportant source material should not need to leave a team’s environment to be useful.
  • Policies people can readCritical guardrails belong in reviewable files, not hidden product settings.
